# Initial Rumour Catalogue

Twenty provisional rumours for Grimoire's opening turns. Costs, thresholds,
durations, and rewards need playtesting. These rumours develop a wizard's
resources and assets; none awards victory progress.

## Catalogue conventions

- Each wizard's rumour mill has one instance of each eligible rumour design.
  Active rumours and rumours reserved by Investigate are unavailable to draw.
  All drawable rumours are equally likely. Discarded rumours return to the mill.
- Eligibility uses current Affinity for a normal draw. Investigate also uses
  its assigned Minions' Expertise and committed support. Every listed domain
  is required; the catalogue has no alternative eligibility requirements.
- Duration counts the turn a rumour enters play. A rumour reserved by
  Investigate starts its duration when played on the following turn.
- All rumours are mandatory to play. Every Challenge needs a Challenger,
  even when its only printed requirement is a resource payment. The listed
  requirements are the complete additional requirements for that Challenge.
- A Challenge uses current Affinity plus matching assigned Expertise and
  committed support. Arcane Focus can serve as Challenger and supplies 1 Wis
  plus 1 in a chosen domain. An Element may support that same Challenge.
  Each card is used once that turn; Affinity is reusable.
- Success discards the rumour immediately. When alternatives are offered,
  succeeding at one grants only that alternative's reward. Only Burn the Witch
  has a harmful Failure effect; every other unsuccessful Challenge simply
  loses its opportunity at expiry. Solar Eclipse has no Challenge or Failure.
- Gained cards are fresh copies placed in the wizard's discard pile. There
  is no reward supply limit in this provisional catalogue. Rewards must later
  be drawn and played; gained Minions cannot undertake Challenges on the turn
  they enter play.
- Basic and generated ephemeral resource cards cost zero and supply one unit
  of their named resource. Direct resource grants are available immediately
  and expire at turn end if unspent.
- A two-turn Affinity reward starts immediately on Success and expires at the
  end of the wizard's following turn. It survives the rumour's discard and is
  present at the next turn's rumour draw. Contributions stack normally.

## Affinity-free rumours

### 1. Promising Pupil

A stranger with an unusual gift is looking for a teacher.

- **Eligibility:** None. **Duration:** 2 turns.
- **Challenge — Accept the Pupil:** Commit Arcane Focus and pay 3 Wis total.
  Focus supplies 1 of that Wis, leaving 2 to pay from available resources.
- **Success:** Create a new Pupil Minion and put it in the discard pile.
  Choose its sole Expertise domain uniformly from the eight domains other
  than the Element of this wizard's drafted Chantry; its rank is 1.
- **Failure:** No additional effect.

Every successful recruitment creates a separate Pupil. Generate its domain
once at that Success; it remains fixed for the entire game, including after
discarding, drawing, and replaying the card. Pupils may share a domain. Losing
the drafted Chantry does not change the excluded domain. This is an Expertise
domain, with no passive Affinity: it can broaden Investigate's draw and help
overcome Challenges, while lasting ordinary eligibility needs an Affinity
source. See the Pupil reward definition below.

### 2. Solar Eclipse

For a few precious hours, shadows reveal workings hidden in ordinary daylight.

- **Eligibility:** None. **Duration:** 1 turn.
- **On play:** Gain 2 available Wis, Fire 1, and Infernal 1 immediately.
- **Challenge:** None.
- **Expiry:** The Affinity contributions expire at turn end; unspent Wis also
  expires. Return the rumour to the mill without Success or Failure.

The Affinities can support this turn's actions and Investigate. They expire
before the next normal rumour draw.

### 3. Burn the Witch

A frightened crowd has cornered a practitioner who might become your ally.

- **Eligibility:** None. **Duration:** 3 turns.
- **Challenge — Arrest the Witch:** Might 1.
  **Success:** End the hunt with no further reward.
- **Alternative Challenge — Recruit the Witch:** Pay 2 Wis.
  **Success:** Gain a Witch Minion in the discard pile.
- **Failure:** At the end of the third turn, choose one Asset you control and
  discard it once. If you control no Assets, discard nothing.

There are no recurring penalties. Either successful Challenge removes the
rumour and prevents its Failure effect.

### 4. New Leyline

A newly exposed current of magic could sustain a place of study.

- **Eligibility:** None. **Duration:** 2 turns.
- **Challenge — Claim the Leyline:** Pay 2 Wis.
- **Success:** Gain a Leyline Location in the discard pile.
- **Failure:** No additional effect.

### 5. Full Moon

Something valuable can be found beneath the moon, if you dare the hunt.

- **Eligibility:** None. **Duration:** 1 turn.
- **Challenge — Moonlit Hunt:** Might 1. Attempt after the mandatory rumour
  plays and before other optional actions, with eligible support. This timing
  restriction applies only to the hunt.
- **Success:** Gain one basic Wealth card in the discard pile. Immediately
  gain Water 1 and Infernal 1 for two turns, including this turn.
- **Failure:** No additional effect.

### 6. Wandering Merchant

A travelling trader offers a profitable exchange of coin and curious knowledge.

- **Eligibility:** None. **Duration:** 2 turns.
- **Challenge — Strike a Bargain:** Pay 1 Wealth.
- **Success:** Gain one basic Wis card and one basic Wealth card in the
  discard pile.
- **Failure:** No additional effect.

### 7. Lost Courier

A courier carrying sealed court letters has vanished from the road.

- **Eligibility:** None. **Duration:** 2 turns.
- **Challenge — Follow the Messages:** Air 1.
  **Success:** Gain a Court Office Location in the discard pile.
- **Alternative Challenge — Escort the Courier:** Might 1.
  **Success:** Gain one basic Wealth card in the discard pile and immediately
  gain Nobility 1 for two turns, including this turn.
- **Failure:** No additional effect.

### 8. Village Petition

The village offers a meeting place to anyone who will restore its neglected well.

- **Eligibility:** None. **Duration:** 2 turns.
- **Challenge — Rally the Villagers:** Popularity 1 and pay 1 Wis.
- **Alternative Challenge — Repair the Well:** Earth 1 and pay 1 Wis.
- **Success of either Challenge:** Gain a Village Hall Location in the
  discard pile.
- **Failure:** No additional effect.

## Reward cards

All assets below are durable, require no Affinity to play, have no upkeep,
and have no abilities beyond those listed. Costs are payments made when
playing the gained card from the hand, separate from the recruitment or
claiming Challenge. Multiple copies are allowed, and their Affinities stack.
An exhausted Minion continues supplying its passive Affinity.

| Reward card | Category | Play cost | Passive Affinity | Expertise | Recurring income |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pupil | Minion | 1 Wis | None | One fixed random domain at 1 | None |
| Witch | Minion | 1 Wis | None | Infernal 1 | None |
| Leyline | Location | 1 Wis | None | None | One ephemeral Wis each turn |
| Court Office | Location | 1 Wis | Nobility 1 | None | None |
| Village Hall | Location | 1 Wis | Popularity 1 | None | None |
| Stone Circle | Location | 1 Wis | Earth 1 | None | None |
| Healing Spring | Location | 1 Wis | Water 1 | None | None |
| Abbey Liaison | Minion | 2 Wis | Church 1 | Church 1 | None |
| Crossroads Shrine | Location | 1 Wis | Infernal 1 | None | None |
| Caravan Guard | Minion | 2 Wis | Might 1 | Might 1 | None |
| Ember Shrine | Location | 2 Wis | Fire 1, Infernal 1 | None | None |
| Chartered Quarry | Location | 3 Wis | Earth 1, Nobility 1 | None | One ephemeral Wealth each turn |
| Reedbound Healer | Minion | 2 Wis | Church 1 | Water 1, Church 1 | None |

Recurring income starts at the beginning of the turn after the asset enters
play. Each generated ephemeral card costs zero and provides one named resource
when played; it is destroyed at that turn's end, played or unused. The starting
Chantry is already in play during setup and generates its first Wis card on
turn one. Direct Affinity contributions begin as soon as an asset enters play.

Basic Wis and basic Wealth rewards are ordinary, non-ephemeral resource cards:
each costs zero, provides one unit when played, and follows normal discard
and retention rules.

## Opening coverage and availability

The eight unrestricted rumours remain eligible even if the Chantry is lost.
Each starting Chantry adds its corresponding single-domain rumour to ordinary
draws. Every starting Minion's Expertise has a corresponding single-domain
rumour it can unlock through Investigate, and can help meet Challenges in that
domain. A Pupil expands that choice into a domain other than the drafted Element.

For example, an Earth wizard assigning Leontine Grange to Investigate has
Earth 1 from the Chantry and Nobility 1 from Leontine's Expertise. That attempt
can draw Disputed Inheritance or The Baron's Forgotten Quarry as well as the
unrestricted and Earth rumours. The drawn rumour is reserved for next turn
without an eligibility recheck. Its later Challenge still needs its own
Challenger and requirements met afresh.

Persistent reward sources cover Nobility, Church, Infernal, Might, and
Popularity. Temporary rewards offer earlier access to Nobility, Popularity,
Water, and Infernal. These sources allow ordinary draws to broaden after
opportunities first found through Investigate.

With the starting deck's one Investigate, a wizard draws at most two rumours
per turn. Durations never exceed three turns, so at most six rumours are
unavailable immediately before an Investigate draw: up to two incoming rumours
on each of the last three turns. Eight unrestricted designs therefore leave
at least two drawable even when all Affinity is lost and no Challenge succeeds.
On turn one there is no prior reservation, so turns 1–3 can occupy at most
five designs before the third turn's Investigate draw. Successful Challenges
and shorter durations return designs sooner.

This availability check covers the initial deck and this catalogue, which
creates no additional Investigate cards. Future content permitting more
Investigate uses or longer-lived rumours must revisit the availability bound;
this catalogue introduces no new global draw cap or empty-mill rule.

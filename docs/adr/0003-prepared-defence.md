# Prepare defence before interaction resolves

The requirement to choose all defence before turn submission is superseded by
[Allow player responses to directed actions](0010-allow-player-responses.md).
Defensive preparation may still exist alongside responses; its exact role is
open. The goal of distinct defensive tools across strategies is retained.

Because player-to-player impacts resolve after turn submission without response
decisions, wizards combine modest standing protection with deliberate defensive
commitments of cards or Minions made before submission.
This makes defence a preparation choice that competes with other uses of those
cards, rather than relying solely on whatever capacity happens to remain.
Different strategies should provide distinct defensive tools so holding off an
Aggressor does not require every wizard to build the same military engine.

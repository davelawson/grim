# Direct attacks use Might

Every Minion with Might Expertise has an Attack action available without
requiring a separate attack card to be drawn, and several such Minions may
combine into the wizard's single attack for the turn, making group commitment
a trade-off against defence and other Challenges.
Each side totals its wizard's Might Affinity and the Might Expertise of its
committed Minions, plus 1 Might if Arcane Focus supports that side; the attack
succeeds only if attacking Might exceeds defending Might by at least 2, and
success always reduces Integrity by 1 rather than choosing an asset or resource
objective.
Attacking and defending Minions must be ready and exhaust when committed to
their pending assignment, retaining surviving contributions; newly played Minions may
defend immediately but cannot Attack or undertake Challenges until their
owner's next turn starts.

## Arcane Focus support

Focus may support an Attack or Defence with 1 Might instead of its other uses.
Once played, it must be regained before it can be used again; the earlier
once-per-round restriction is superseded by
[the owner-turn recovery model](0011-round-robin-turns.md).
An immune Target cannot be attacked.

## Failed attacks

On an unsuccessful attack, the attacker chooses one of the committed attacking
Minions to discard, if possible, in addition to the ordinary exhaustion of
participants; uncommitted passive Might sources and Arcane Focus are not
eligible for this loss. An interrupted or countered Attack still attempts to
resolve; if it fails, this normal penalty applies when a committed attacking
Minion remains eligible to discard.
This is the established Spent outcome: the Asset goes to its owner's discard
pile and must be drawn and played again, rather than being destroyed.

## Attack resolution

The original rule fixed every attack and defence total before applying any
combat losses. Defensive Spells may now remove an attacking participant before
the Might comparison, as with Firewall; other effects may apply afterward,
as with a moat that prolongs exhaustion.
Attacks now resolve within their active wizard's turn using
[the response protocol](0012-response-windows-and-latest-first-resolution.md),
rather than waiting for a shared post-submission phase. Removed participants
do not contribute at resolution, and a failed interrupted Attack applies the
normal unsuccessful-Attack penalty.

The attacker chooses and discards a surviving committed attacking Minion as
part of the unsuccessful Attack's uninterrupted resolution, before the next
response window opens.

# Open response windows and resolve pending effects latest-first

An exchange can now end early when a wizard reaches five-step victory after an
uninterrupted resolution, as specified by
[Immediate victory and Challenge advancement](0016-immediate-victory-and-challenge-advancement.md).
The remaining response protocol below is retained.

Every ordinary action opens a response window after declaration and before
resolution, while payments and bookkeeping do not create separate windows.
Actions and Reactions remain pending, with the latest response resolving first,
so a counter can prevent a defensive effect before the original action resolves.
The active wizard finishes the entire exchange before taking another ordinary
action.

Eligible participants are fixed for the original action: the active wizard and
their two neighbours, or the wider group specified by a global action. Priority
rotates in turn order starting after the latest declarer; a new Reaction resets
the pass count, and consecutive passes from everyone resolve the newest effect.
After resolution, Priority returns to the active wizard if effects remain; an
empty Stack ends the exchange. Passing declines only the current opportunity.

Only expressly permitted Reactions and defensive commitments are available
during a response window. Cards are committed and costs paid at declaration,
including resource cards played as part of payment without separate windows;
countering an effect leaves those cards and resources spent. This makes response
order explicit and prevents several pending effects from promising the same
resources.

Minions exhaust when committed and retain surviving assigned contributions;
countering leaves them exhausted in play. Costs and play requirements are
checked at declaration, while Challenge capacity, participants, and targets
are rechecked against the surviving state at resolution. Each action attempts
to resolve regardless; if it fails, it counts as a failure and normal failure
penalties apply at their printed timing. The earlier blanket exemption for
interrupted actions is replaced; a rumour's explicit expiry penalty is not
applied early for a failed attempt and can still be avoided by timely Success.

Mandatory effects triggered during resolution enter the Stack before its
empty check; the active wizard chooses and reveals the order of simultaneous
triggers; if that wizard has been eliminated, the next living wizard in Ring
order chooses instead. Eliminated players leave Priority rotation immediately,
while their
pending effects remain subject to normal checks. Finish the exchange before
ending an eliminated active wizard's turn, without drawing them another hand.
A global Reaction does not expand the eligible participants. Neighbours may
respond to ordinary actions; only an original global action admits a wider
group of responders.

Active-wizard elimination is a future contingency; no currently defined card
or Attack rule causes it during that wizard's own turn.

Each pending effect resolves as one uninterrupted operation, performing its
remaining legal instructions and applying normal failure consequences if its
success requirements are unmet. Challenge rewards require all requirements;
unsuccessful Attack losses are chosen and applied during that resolution,
before the next response window.

Mandatory card effects also use the Stack and allow responses, with each
exchange completed before the next required effect. Recovery, income-card
generation, upkeep payment, and cleanup do not independently open windows.
Available resources, including those generated by responders, expire at the
end of the current active wizard's turn.

Entering the ending phase is final. Mandatory end-of-turn exchanges may still
permit Reactions, but new cards or opportunities do not let the active wizard
resume ordinary actions before cleanup and drawing their next hand.

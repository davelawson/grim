# Immediate victory and Challenge advancement

Successful Victory Challenges immediately award their visible progress and
replace the completed card with its next rank in the discard pile; reaching
five steps instead ends the game immediately, even during a turn. This
supersedes the former round-end advancement and victory checkpoint, so later
wizards do not receive a catch-up turn after someone reaches the winning
threshold.

Finish the single uninterrupted effect or kneeling decision that awards the
winning progress, then end the game without resolving remaining pending
effects, mandatory triggers, End Phase steps, or a handoff. Last-survivor
victory instead waits for the whole exchange to finish, preserving the
opportunity for its remaining effects to eliminate the survivor too; zero
survivors at that checkpoint produce a draw. Five-step victory can end the
game before that later check.

If the same uninterrupted effect leaves multiple surviving contenders at the
winning threshold, retain the former comparison of other paths at that
checkpoint: set aside one winning path, sort each contender's remaining path
progress descending, and compare values in order. More progress at the first
difference wins; contenders still tied share victory, without another turn
or response.

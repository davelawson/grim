# Use Integrity for sustained pressure

To give a Target multiple opportunities to address aggression, Integrity starts
at 3 and measures their personal protections: only their Aggressor can attack
them directly, launching at most one attack on their Target per turn and
reducing the Target's Integrity by at most 1 through that attack.
Reaching zero Integrity leaves a wizard in play, but any further Integrity
reduction eliminates them; weakening Integrity grants immunity to attacks for
the next turn, including its deferred attack resolution, providing a guaranteed
interval to address the aggression.
Recovery is a built-in alternative use of Arcane Focus with a significant cost
and requires Focus to be drawn normally, preserving the importance of the
wizard's hand and competing uses of Focus; it restores 1 Integrity up to a
maximum of 3 and costs 3 Wis total, with Focus contributing 1 Wis and the
wizard paying the remaining 2 Wis.

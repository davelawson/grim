# Restrict in-play visibility to neighbours

Superseded by [Full table information with hidden private zones](0014-full-table-information.md).
The text below records the earlier decision.

A wizard's Aggressor and Target can see everything that wizard has in play,
while other players see only their progress on the Victory paths.
Exposed information is updated only after each turn rather than during actions
or assignments, so combat preparation does not produce live updates.
This gives immediate rivals the information needed to plan while limiting
information available across the wider Ring.
